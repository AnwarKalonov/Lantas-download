Double-click the downloaded Lantas-1.0.6-arm64.dmg. A Finder window opens with the app on the left and Applications on the right. Drag Lantas across, eject the disk image, then launch it from Applications or Spotlight.

The macOS warning

Why macOS says it “could not verify” Lantas

Lantas is signed ad-hoc rather than with a paid Apple Developer ID, and it is not notarized by Apple. macOS therefore cannot confirm the developer the first time you open it and shows “Apple could not verify ‘Lantas’ is free of malware”. That warning is expected for this independent build; it does not mean the download is damaged or that Lantas needs special access.

The release is built by the project’s automated macOS workflow and published on the Lantas release page. The app source stays private; each download is the exact build from that release, with no installer extras or bundled offers.

  1. 01
    Open System Settings → Privacy & Security.
  2. 02
    Scroll to Security. Next to the Lantas message, click Open Anyway.
  3. 03
    Enter your Mac password, then click Open. Lantas opens normally from then on.
If the Open Anyway button is not there, open Lantas from Applications once more, then return to this panel.
Prefer Terminal? Clear the quarantine flag
After dragging Lantas into Applications, run this once. It removes the download quarantine attribute from that copy only; it does not disable Gatekeeper. 
xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/Lantas.app
The repository also includes an install script that copies the app and clears the flag for you.
What not to do
Do not run spctl --master-disable or turn Gatekeeper off for the whole Mac. Approving this one app in Privacy & Security is enough, and it keeps the protection for everything else.
The app says it is damaged
Eject the disk image, delete the copy in Applications, and download the DMG again from the link on this page. If it still fails, open an issue with your macOS version and the exact message; do not work around it by disabling system security.

After it opens

Two minutes

Lantas is usable without an account. Add a model key in Settings, or point it at a local model, then open the Browser tab. The Chrome extension is optional; it lets Lantas work in the Chrome window you already have open, with your existing logins.

  1. Connect a modelSettings → Providers. Anthropic, OpenAI, Google, NVIDIA NIM, OpenRouter, Groq, Ollama or LM Studio.
  2. Open the workspaceChoose a folder for Code, or ask in Chat and switch to the Browser tab.
  3. Add the extensionOpen chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode, and load the folder shown in Lantas.
